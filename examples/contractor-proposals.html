Call notes to a priced proposal for a renovation contractor

This is an example project. Atlantic Ave Renovations, its staff and its client are made up. The Claude output on this page comes from a test run in Claude and hasn't been edited.

Business
Renovation contractor in Brooklyn, 8 people, mostly kitchens and apartment remodels
Package
Workflow Build, $6,000, four weeks
Software they use
Google Workspace and QuickBooks

What was taking the time

The owner does every site visit and types notes on a phone while walking the job. At night the owner turns those notes into a proposal, looking up each price in a spreadsheet and copying the layout from an old proposal. A typical one takes an hour or more, and there are four to six a week.

Proposals often go out three or four days after the visit. Homeowners who are getting several bids sometimes sign with whoever answers first.

What we would set up

  • Claude Team for the owner, the office manager and the lead carpenter, with training on their data turned off
  • A shared Claude Project with the 2026 price list, three past proposals and instructions to use only listed prices. Anything that isn't on the price list goes in a "To confirm" section instead of getting a guessed price.
  • The automation: notes saved to a "Site visits" folder in Google Drive become a draft proposal in a "To review" folder, and the office manager gets an email when it's ready
  • A Claude Skill for change orders that uses the same price list
  • Training for the owner and office manager, and a one-page policy on what goes into Claude
  • Time from site visit to proposal sent, measured before we start and again at handoff

Nothing goes to a homeowner until the owner or office manager has read the draft and sent it.

The test run

We built the Project in Claude with a made-up price list and past proposal, then pasted in these notes.

What we sent

site visit 10/28 - 88 Bergen st #3, Marcus T kitchen/dining wall coming out, ~14 ft, NOT load bearing per super patch + paint after. dining room walls ~380sqft, ceiling ~200 wants 4 recessed lights in dining, 1 extra outlet by window floor - LVP in dining, ~200 sqft, floor uneven near radiator, needs leveling will need dumpster, prob 1 load asked about permit - yes file it also asked about moving the radiator?? not sure, check w plumber wants to start before thanksgiving Draft a proposal from these notes.

What Claude wrote

The total is $8,077, with nine line items from the price list plus 10% project management. Please check a few things before you send it:

  • Radiator move: I listed it under "To confirm" because it isn't on the price list.
  • Drywall patching: I didn't put a price on this, because the notes don't say how many square feet. It's under "To confirm" at $4.25/sq ft.
  • Floor leveling: I priced it for the whole 200 sq ft, but the notes only mention the area near the radiator. If only that spot needs leveling, the price goes down.
  • Date: Your notes say the site visit is 10/28, which is after today's date, so I dated the proposal October 25. Change it if needed.
  • Start date: A DOB permit could make starting before Thanksgiving tight.

To confirm

  • Radiator relocation: not on price list. Needs plumber review before pricing.
  • Drywall patching where the wall comes out: area to be measured on site, priced at $4.25/sq ft.
  • Wall is non-load-bearing per building super; to be verified before demolition.
  • Floor leveling priced across the full 200 sq ft; may be less if only the area near the radiator needs it.
  • Paint colors and LVP selection, chosen by client before start.

Claude wrote the proposal as a document, which also has scope, timeline and terms sections. The line items and To confirm list are copied from it.

What a person still checks

The owner measures the drywall patch, gets a plumber's price for the radiator and decides whether the whole floor needs leveling, and only then does the proposal go out. In this run, every price matched the price list and the math added up, which we check on every test case before handoff.
