Month-end client emails for a bookkeeping firm

This is an example. Tompkins & Vale Bookkeeping, its staff, its client and the transactions are made up. The AI output on this page is from a test run in Claude, the AI we use, and hasn’t been edited.

Business
Bookkeeping firm in Brooklyn, 12 people, about 140 small business clients
Package
AI Operations, from $12,000, eight weeks
Software they use
QuickBooks Online and Google Workspace

Where the time went

At Tompkins & Vale, the first week of every month goes to chasing clients. Each bookkeeper goes through their clients’ unsorted bank and card charges and emails each client about them. With about 140 clients, that’s 140 emails a month.

The replies come back as photos, forwarded receipts and one-line answers. A client’s month can’t close until someone matches those replies to the right charges.

What we’d set up

  • A company AI account for all 12 staff. It doesn’t learn from client data, and it stays on the firm’s own account.
  • A policy for a firm that handles other people’s money. Use the last four digits only for card and account numbers, and never ask clients to email Social Security numbers or passwords.
  • A “Month-end emails” workspace with the firm’s email guide, a past email the team liked and the chart of accounts
  • Automation one. On the 1st of each month, each client’s unsorted charges come out of QuickBooks Online. A draft email then lands in their bookkeeper’s Gmail.
  • Automation two. Client replies with receipts get labeled by client in Gmail, so they’re easy to match.
  • Automation three. Every Monday, the partners get a list of clients who haven’t replied.
  • Each automation tested on 20 to 50 past months of the firm’s own data before it goes live, plus training for everyone

Nothing goes to a client on its own. The bookkeeper reads each draft, edits it if needed and sends it.

We tried it

We set up the month-end emails workspace with a made-up email guide, past email and chart of accounts. Then we pasted in one client’s October charges.

What the AI wrote back

Subject: October books, 8 quick questions

Hi Maya,

We're closing October for Corner Kettle Coffee and need your help with 8 items.

Receipts we need

  • Oct 6, COSTCO WHSE #1080, $412.19 (card ending 4417). Could you send the receipt? A photo is fine.
  • Oct 28, HOME DEPOT #6177, $139.56 (card ending 4417). Could you send the receipt?

Questions

  • Oct 2, AMZN MKTP US*2K7LP0, $86.42. What was this order? A screenshot of the order page works too.
  • Oct 3, VENMO *DANIEL O, $350.00. What was this payment for?
  • Oct 9, SQ *GREENPOINT DAIRY, $268.00, twice on the same day. Were you charged twice? If so, it's worth checking with Greenpoint Dairy about a refund.
  • Oct 14, ONLINE TRANSFER TO CHK ...7781, $1,840.00. Which account is the one ending in 7781?
  • Oct 17, NETFLIX.COM, $22.99. What is this subscription used for?
  • Oct 21, DEPOSIT MOBILE, $615.00. What was this deposit for?

If you can reply by November 10, we'll have October closed that week.

Thanks,
Luis

INTERNAL - DON'T SEND

  • 10/02 AMZN MKTP $86.42: 6900 Ask client until we hear back. Likely 6300 Office supplies or 5020 Packaging and to-go supplies.
  • 10/03 VENMO *DANIEL O $350.00: 6900 for now. If it was for work done, use 6100 Contract labor and track Daniel for a possible 1099.
  • 10/06 COSTCO #1080 $412.19: Likely 5000 Food and beverage. The receipt may show a split with 5020 or 6300.
  • 10/09 GREENPOINT DAIRY $268.00 x2: 5010 Dairy. Possible duplicate. Same vendor, amount and date. Book one charge and hold the second until Maya confirms.
  • 10/14 TRANSFER TO CHK ...7781 $1,840.00: If 7781 is another business account, use 1050 Transfer between accounts. If it's personal, use 3100 Owner draw. Flag as a transfer.
  • 10/17 NETFLIX.COM $22.99: Looks personal. Likely 3100 Owner draw. Use 6400 Software and subscriptions only if Maya says it's used in the cafe.
  • 10/21 DEPOSIT MOBILE $615.00: Likely 4000 Sales (for example a check from a catering or wholesale customer) or 4900 Other income. If it came from the 7781 account, use 1050.
  • 10/28 HOME DEPOT #6177 $139.56: 6200 Repairs and maintenance or 6210 Small equipment and tools, depending on the receipt.
  • Date check: The Home Depot charge is dated 10/28, two days after today (10/26). It may be pending or mis-dated in the export. Confirm it posts before closing October.

What Luis still does

Luis reads the email before it goes to Maya and decides how to book each charge once she answers. The categories in the internal note are a starting point. In this setup the AI doesn’t change anything in QuickBooks.
