Example projects

What we’d set up for three kinds of small business, one for each of our main packages, with a test run in Claude for each.

These businesses and people are made up. We set each one up in Claude with made-up price lists, emails and transactions, and Claude’s output on each page is unedited. Once clients agree to it, their projects will go here too.

Atlantic Ave Renovations

Renovation contractor, 8 people
Workflow Build, $6,000

Notes typed at a site visit become a priced proposal in the contractor’s own format. Anything that isn’t on the price list is left for the owner to price.

In the test: nine line items, an $8,077 total and five things to check before sending.
