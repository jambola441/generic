Who we work with

Most owners we talk to already use ChatGPT or Claude themselves. What they want is the whole team using it, working from the same documents, with clear rules about customer data.

Good fit

  • You have 2 to 50 people, in or near New York City
  • You use AI already and want your staff using it too
  • Your team writes a lot: quotes, proposals, customer emails, intake forms, review replies
  • You run something like a law, accounting or insurance office, a brokerage, a contracting company, an agency, a shop or a restaurant group
  • You'd like AI connected to software you already use, like your CRM or QuickBooks

Probably not a fit

  • Medical and dental offices. Patient data needs a different setup, so we quote it separately.
  • You want things sent, posted or paid with nobody checking first. We don't build that.
  • Website chatbots. We don't build them, but we can point you to someone who does.
  • One-person businesses. Anthropic, the company behind Claude, runs free AI workshops in NYC, and they're a better place to start.

What we set up

Here's what we usually do.

Your account

A company AI account for your team. It doesn't learn from your data, and you decide who signs in and what it connects to. We also write a one-page policy on what's okay to put into it.

Your documents

We load your price lists, procedures, templates and past writing into shared workspaces. Then the AI's answers use your numbers and sound like you.

Training

Hands-on sessions using your team's own work. We record them and write a short how-to for each task.

Automations

Work that happens without anyone typing a prompt. For example, call notes turned into a priced proposal, new leads logged with a draft reply, or a weekly nudge about overdue invoices.

Your data, and what we promise

What happens to our data?

We set you up on Claude, made by Anthropic, using its business plan, never personal accounts. Anthropic doesn't train its models on data from business plans, and you decide what the AI can connect to.

Your one-page policy spells out what can go into the AI and what can't. Connections start read-only, so the AI can look but can't change anything. We add more access only if you say so.

If you handle patient health information, the setup is different. Get in touch and we'll tell you whether we can help.

What do you promise?

For a Workflow Build or AI Operations, if the automation isn't running within 60 days of kickoff, we keep working on it for free. We also fix problems in anything we built, free, for 90 days.

AI makes mistakes. So every automation has someone on your team approve its work before it reaches a customer, your books or your bank. We can't promise how much time you'll save, but we measure it before and after so you can see for yourself.

FAQ

Who does the work?

generic.tech's founder, a software engineer in Brooklyn. Before this, generic.tech did software architecture and reliability work, so we build the connections ourselves.

Which AI do you use?

Claude, made by Anthropic. It's the one we know best, its business plan doesn't use your data for training, and it connects to the software most small businesses already use. If you already pay for ChatGPT, we'll tell you whether switching is worth it.

Do we need technical staff?

No. Your team uses the AI's regular app on the web, a computer or a phone. If anything needs code, we write it and leave notes on what runs where.

How much of our team's time will this take?

For Kickstart, plan on about a day of the owner's time over the two weeks, mostly the half-day review and pulling documents together. Everyone else spends 90 minutes in training. A Workflow Build adds about an hour a week from whoever does that task, to test it and tell us what to change. AI Operations needs the same from one person on each team involved.

What else will we pay for?

The AI subscription, billed to you. We use Claude Team, which costs $25 per person per month with a two-person minimum. It comes with monthly credits that usually cover the automations we build. If you already pay for Zapier or Make, we may use it. Most clients spend under $100 a month on software for this.

Why fixed prices?

So you know the cost before we start. The agreement lists exactly what's included, and anything extra is quoted in writing first.

What if AI isn't a good fit for our business?

The Kickstart report will say so, and why. Businesses that do most of their work by phone or in person, with little written down, usually get less out of AI.

How is this different from using ChatGPT?

Your staff probably already use ChatGPT on their own. We set up one shared account with your documents and write the rules for using it. We also build automations that run without anyone typing a prompt. And we connect it to software you use, like Google Workspace, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Shopify, Square and Slack.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It's all built on your accounts. We may reuse our general templates and methods with other clients, but nothing specific to your business.

Is there funding available?

Maybe. NYC Small Business Services has a Customized Training Grant that pays back part of training costs for NYC businesses training five or more people. The training in Kickstart and Workflow Build may qualify, and we can help you apply.
