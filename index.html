Who this is for

We do our best work with teams that already ship and want to ship faster, with fewer surprises.

A good fit

  • Product companies with a working system and an engineering team of roughly 3 to 40
  • Teams where every change takes longer than it used to, and nobody can say exactly why
  • Systems with recurring incidents, unclear ownership, or a cloud bill that grew faster than the business
  • Founders and CTOs who want a senior second opinion before a rewrite, a hire, or a raise

Not a fit

  • Building a product from scratch. We make existing systems better; we are not an agency.
  • Staff augmentation or a body to fill a seat on your sprint board
  • Round-the-clock on-call. We help you build the team and tooling that handles it.

Questions we get asked

Do you write code or just advise?

Both. The audit is advisory and ends in a written report. The implementation partnership is hands-on: we work in your repositories alongside your engineers, and the point is that they can carry it on without us.

Remote or on site?

We are based in Brooklyn and work remotely by default. For NYC teams we are happy to be on site for kickoffs, audits and the weeks where it helps.

What happens if we stop after the audit?

Nothing. The report is yours, it is written to be actionable by your own team, and plenty of clients use it that way. If you come back later, the audit fee still counts toward the first week.

What stack do you work with?

The problems we fix are mostly about boundaries, data models, testing and operations, which look the same in most languages. Tell us what you run on the call and we will say honestly whether we are the right people.

We're a small business, not a software company. Is this for us?

Probably not this page. For teams of 2 to 50 who want Claude set up safely and wired into the tools they already use, see AI for small business. Same engineer, different packages.

Tell us what's slowing your team down.

Thirty minutes. If we're not the right fit we'll say so and point you somewhere useful.
