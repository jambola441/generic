Who we work with

Most of our clients already use ChatGPT or Claude on their own. They want the whole team using it, working from the same documents, with clear rules about customer data.

Good fit

  • 2 to 50 employees, in or near New York City
  • The owner uses AI already and wants staff using it too
  • Regular written work, like proposals, quotes, customer emails, intake forms, bookkeeping prep, scheduling or review replies
  • Law, accounting and insurance offices, real estate brokerages, contractors, agencies, retailers and restaurant groups
  • You want it connected to your CRM, accounting or booking software

Probably not a fit

  • Medical and dental offices with patient data. HIPAA rules out the standard Claude Team plan, so this needs a separate quote.
  • Automations that pay, send or post with no person checking first
  • Website chatbots. We don't build them, but we can recommend someone who does.
  • One-person businesses. Anthropic runs free Claude for Small Business workshops in NYC, and they're a better place to start.

What we set up

Most engagements cover these four areas.

Account setup

A Claude Team account on your company domain, with training on your data turned off, spending limits, and only the integrations you approve. We also write a one-page policy on what staff can put into Claude and who checks its output.

Your documents

Shared Claude Projects with your procedures, price lists, templates and writing samples, so answers use your numbers and sound like your business.

Training

Hands-on sessions using your team's own tasks. We record the sessions and write up instructions for each workflow.

Automations

For example, new leads logged in your CRM with a draft reply, or call notes turned into a priced proposal. Others handle overdue invoice reminders or inbox sorting. A staff member approves each result before it goes out.

Data and guarantees

What happens to our data?

We only set clients up on Claude Team or Enterprise plans, never personal accounts. Anthropic doesn't train its models on data from those plans, and your admin decides which integrations Claude can use.

Every engagement includes a one-page policy listing what can go into Claude, what can't, and who reviews output before it leaves the business. Integrations start with read-only access. We add write access only with your approval.

Patient health information requires a different setup. Get in touch and we'll tell you whether we can help.

What do you guarantee?

For Workflow Build and AI Operations, if the agreed workflow isn't running within 60 days of kickoff, we keep working on it at no charge. We also fix problems in anything we delivered, free, for 90 days.

Claude makes mistakes. Every workflow we build has a person approve the output before it reaches a customer, your books or your bank. We don't promise specific savings, but we measure time spent before and after so you can judge the results.

FAQ

Who does the work?

generic.tech's founder, a software engineer. Before focusing on small businesses, generic.tech did software architecture and reliability work, so we build the integrations ourselves.

Do we need technical staff?

No. Everything is managed through Claude's own apps. We write any code that's needed and leave written instructions explaining what runs where.

How much of our team's time will this take?

For Kickstart, plan on about a day of the owner's time over the two weeks, mostly the half-day review and gathering documents, plus 90 minutes from each person in the training session. A Workflow Build adds about an hour a week from whoever runs that workflow, to test the results and tell us what to change. AI Operations needs the same from a point person on each team involved.

What will we pay Anthropic, and what else do we need to buy?

Claude Team costs $25 per seat per month, with a two-seat minimum, billed directly to you. It includes monthly API credits, which usually cover the automations we build. If you already pay for Zapier or Make, we may use it. Most clients spend under $100 a month on software for this.

Why fixed prices?

So you know the cost before we start. The agreement lists exactly what's included, and any additional work is quoted in writing first.

What if AI isn't a good fit for our business?

The Kickstart report will say so, with reasons. Businesses that do most of their work by phone or in person, with few written records, usually get less out of Claude.

How is this different from using ChatGPT?

Most of our clients' staff already use ChatGPT on their own. We set up a shared account with your documents, write a usage policy, build automations that run without anyone typing a prompt, and connect Claude to software you already use, like Google Workspace, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Shopify, Square and Slack.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is built on your accounts. We may reuse our general templates and methods with other clients, but nothing specific to your business.

Is there funding available?

Possibly. NYC Small Business Services offers a Customized Training Grant that reimburses part of employee training costs for NYC businesses training five or more people. The training in Kickstart and Workflow Build may qualify, and we can help with the application.
