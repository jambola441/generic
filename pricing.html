Who this is for

Most small businesses already use ChatGPT or Claude. Almost none have it running across the team, on real workflows, with rules about what goes in. That gap is the work.

A good fit

  • NYC businesses with 2 to 50 people
  • An owner who already uses AI personally and wants the team to catch up
  • At least one repetitive, text-heavy workflow: proposals and quotes, customer email, intake, bookkeeping prep, scheduling, reviews, content
  • Law, accounting and insurance firms, real estate brokerages, contractors and home services, agencies, e-commerce and specialty retail, restaurant groups
  • You want someone who can actually wire it into QuickBooks, your CRM or your booking system, not just run a class

Not a fit (yet)

  • Medical and dental offices handling patient data on a standard Claude Team plan. HIPAA needs a different setup. We can scope it, but it is not one of the packages below.
  • Anyone who wants AI to move money or send to customers with no human approval
  • A chatbot for your website. Different product, and we will point you to someone good.
  • Solo operators. Start with Anthropic's free Claude for Small Business workshops and come back when you hire.

Data, accuracy, and what we promise

The two questions every owner asks first. Here are the straight answers.

We set clients up on Claude Team or Enterprise, not personal accounts. On those plans Anthropic does not train on your data, and you get admin control over which tools Claude can reach.

Questions owners actually ask

Do we need anyone technical on staff?

No. Everything we set up is managed through Claude's own interface. The only person who needs to write code is us, and we leave written runbooks so a non-technical owner can see what runs where.

What will we pay Anthropic, and what else do we need to buy?

Claude Team is $25 per seat per month, two seats minimum, billed to you directly. The plan includes monthly API credits that cover the cost of most small-business automations. Occasionally a client already pays for Zapier or Make and we use it. Most clients end up under $100 a month in tools, total.

Why fixed prices instead of hourly?

Because you are buying a working result, not our time. Hourly billing rewards slowness and makes you nervous about every email. The hour caps in the agreement exist so that scope stays honest on both sides. If the scope grows, we write a change order first.

What if it turns out AI is not a good fit for us?

Then the Kickstart report says so, in writing, with reasons, and you have spent $2,500 to avoid spending more. It happens. A business that runs on phone calls and handshakes with no written records has less for Claude to work with, and we will tell you that.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

Your team probably already does. The difference is a shared setup with your actual documents and voice loaded in, a policy for what goes in, automations that run without someone typing a prompt, and connections to the tools you already pay for: Google Workspace, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Shopify, Square, Slack and so on. That is the part owners don't have time to figure out alone.

Who owns the prompts, Projects and automations afterward?

You do, outright, on your own accounts. We keep the right to reuse our own general templates and methods with other clients, but nothing specific to your business.

Can you teach us Claude Code or help our developer?

Yes. generic.tech is an engineering consultancy first. If you have a developer or a codebase, that is a different conversation and usually a better-priced one for you. See our engineering engagements.

Can we get help paying for this?

NYC Small Business Services runs a Customized Training Grant that can reimburse a portion of employee training costs for NYC businesses with five or more trainees. The training parts of Kickstart and Workflow Build may qualify. We will help you ask.
